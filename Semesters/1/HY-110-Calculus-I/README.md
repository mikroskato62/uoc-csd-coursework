## 📖 Course Overview

HY-110 introduces rigorous single-variable calculus and real analysis tailored for computer science students. The course emphasizes both analytical problem-solving techniques and formal mathematical proof construction.

## 📋 Course Topics
* **Sequences & Series:** Convergence criteria, Cauchy sequences, limit theorems, Taylor and Maclaurin expansions.
* **Continuity & Differential Calculus:** Epsilon-delta definitions, Mean Value Theorem, L'Hôpital's rule, curve sketching, optimization.
* **Integral Calculus:** Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, techniques of integration (substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions), improper integrals.
* **Applications:** Numerical approximations, power series convergence, differential equations.
