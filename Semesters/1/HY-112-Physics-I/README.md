## 📖 Course Overview

HY-112 covers classical Newtonian mechanics, energy conservation, oscillations, and rotational dynamics, integrated with computational modeling laboratory exercises implemented in Python.

## 📂 Directory Contents

### 1. [`Assignments/`](./Assignments) — Theoretical Problem Sets
Organized into modular subdirectories containing the problem specification and completed solutions:
* [`HW-01/`](./Assignments/HW-01): Vector kinematics, Newton's laws (`Problem-Set-01.pdf`, `Solution-01.pdf`).
* [`HW-02/`](./Assignments/HW-02): Work, energy conservation, and projectile motion.
* [`HW-03/`](./Assignments/HW-03): Momentum, impulse, and collisions.
* [`HW-04/`](./Assignments/HW-04): Rotational dynamics and angular momentum.
* [`HW-05/`](./Assignments/HW-05): Harmonic oscillations and planetary orbits.

### 2. [`Laboratories/`](./Laboratories) — Computational Physics in Python
Hands-on numerical simulations implemented as interactive Jupyter Notebooks:
* [`Lab-01/`](./Laboratories/Lab-01): `HY112-Lab-01.ipynb` — Numerical integration of 1D kinematics and projectile motion with air drag.
* [`Lab-02/`](./Laboratories/Lab-02): `HY112-Lab-02.ipynb` — Harmonic oscillators, phase space trajectories, and damped motion simulation.
* [`Lab-03/`](./Laboratories/Lab-03): `HY112-Lab-03.ipynb` — Driven oscillations and resonance curve analysis.
* [`Lab-04/`](./Laboratories/Lab-04): `HY112-Lab-04.ipynb` — Multi-body orbital mechanics and gravitational simulation.
* [`Lab-05/`](./Laboratories/Lab-05): `HY112-Lab-05.ipynb` — Rigid body dynamics, rotational inertia, and angular momentum.
* [`Lab-06/`](./Laboratories/Lab-06): `HY112-Lab-06.ipynb` — Energy dissipation and coupled oscillator systems.
