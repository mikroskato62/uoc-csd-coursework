## 📖 Course Overview

HY-108 focuses on technical English for Computer Science and academic communication. The curriculum covers foundational technical terminology, reading comprehension of scientific computing papers, listening comprehension, and technical documentation writing.

## 📋 Syllabus & Core Topics
* General technical computing vocabulary and syntax.
* Reading comprehension of computer science texts and technical manuals.
* Academic writing conventions and basic research summarizing.
* Assessment was conducted via oral presentations and written examinations.
