## 📖 Course Overview

HY-120 covers the fundamentals of digital hardware systems, boolean logic, and digital logic circuit design from the gate level up to modular digital subsystems.

## 📋 Syllabus & Key Topics
* **Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates:** Truth tables, Boolean theorems, canonical forms (SOP, POS), NAND/NOR implementations.
* **Combinational Logic Minimization:** Karnaugh maps (2 to 5 variables), Quine-McCluskey method, static and dynamic hazards.
* **Combinational Subsystems:** Adders/Subtractors (Ripple Carry, Carry-Lookahead), ALUs, Encoders, Decoders, Multiplexers, and Demultiplexers.
* **Sequential Logic Design:** Latches and Flip-Flops (SR, D, JK, T), clocking, timing parameters (setup, hold time).
* **Finite State Machines (FSMs):** Mealy and Moore machine models, state minimization, state assignment, synchronous counter and register design.
* Assessment was completed through midterm exams, lab sessions, and final examination.
