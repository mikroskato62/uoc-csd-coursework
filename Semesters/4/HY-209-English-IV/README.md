## 📖 Course Overview

HY-209 is the final capstone course in the technical English curriculum at UoC CSD. It focuses on high-level academic discourse, international conference presentations, peer-review simulation, and professional interview preparation.

## 📋 Key Syllabus Topics
* Scientific literature review writing and bibliography synthesis.
* Preparation of academic slides and delivery of conference-style technical talks.
* Mock technical interviews, executive summaries, and professional portfolio presentation.
* Assessment was completed through oral presentations, written deliverables, and final examination.
