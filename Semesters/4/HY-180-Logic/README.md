## 📖 Course Overview

HY-180 covers formal mathematical logic and automated reasoning foundational to programming language theory, verification, artificial intelligence, database theory, and hardware design.

## 📋 Course Topics

1. **Propositional Logic:**
   * Syntax, semantics, truth assignments, valuations, and satisfaction.
   * Semantic equivalence, validity, satisfiability (SAT), and tautologies.
   * Normal forms: Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF), Disjunctive Normal Form (DNF).
   * Propositional Resolution: Soundness and refutation completeness.
   * Deductive proof systems: Natural Deduction, Gentzen sequents, soundness and completeness theorems.

2. **First-Order (Predicate) Logic:**
   * Syntax: Terms, formulas, free and bound variables, substitutions.
   * Semantics: Structures, interpretations, valuations, truth under a structure.
   * Prenex normal form, Skolemization, and conversion to clausal form.
   * Herbrand's Theorem and First-Order Resolution with Unification (Most General Unifier - MGU).
   * First-Order Natural Deduction, Gödel's Completeness Theorem, and Compactness Theorem.
