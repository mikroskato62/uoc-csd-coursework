## 📖 Course Overview

HY-217 provides the probabilistic and statistical foundation essential for algorithm analysis, randomized data structures, machine learning, information theory, and network modeling.

## 📋 Course Topics
* **Probability Axioms & Combinatorics:** Sample spaces, events, conditional probability, Bayes' Rule, Law of Total Probability, independence.
* **Discrete Random Variables:** Probability Mass Functions (PMF), Cumulative Distribution Functions (CDF), Expectation, Variance, Moments. Distributions: Bernoulli, Binomial, Geometric, Poisson, Hypergeometric.
* **Continuous Random Variables:** Probability Density Functions (PDF), joint distributions, marginal distributions, conditioning. Distributions: Uniform, Exponential, Gaussian (Normal), Gamma.
* **Limit Theorems:** Markov and Chebyshev inequalities, Weak and Strong Laws of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem (CLT).
* **Statistical Inference:** Point estimation, Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE), confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, linear regression.
