## 📖 Course Overview

HY-118 is a cornerstone theoretical course covering discrete structures and mathematical reasoning required for algorithms, data structures, cryptography, and automata theory.

## 📋 Course Topics
* **Mathematical Logic & Proof Methods:** Propositional calculus, predicate logic, direct proofs, proofs by contradiction, contraposition, mathematical and strong induction, structural induction.
* **Sets, Relations & Functions:** Equivalence relations, partial orders, lattices, equivalence classes, injective/surjective/bijective mappings.
* **Combinatorics & Counting:** Multiplication and addition principles, pigeonhole principle, permutations, combinations, binomial coefficients, recurrence relations, generating functions.
* **Graph Theory:** Simple graphs, directed graphs, trees, Eulerian and Hamiltonian graphs, planar graphs, graph coloring (Four Color Theorem concepts), bipartite graphs.
