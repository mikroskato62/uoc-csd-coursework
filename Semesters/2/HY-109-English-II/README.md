## 📖 Course Overview

HY-109 is the second semester of technical academic English for Computer Science. The course builds on HY-108, focusing on technical presentation delivery, technical specification parsing, academic paper reading, and professional written correspondence in software engineering contexts.

## 📋 Key Syllabus Topics
* Advanced technical lexicon in systems programming, networks, and computer hardware.
* Structural analysis of research publications in computer science.
* Oral presentation delivery on emerging computing technologies.
* Assessment was completed through coursework, presentations, and written examinations.
