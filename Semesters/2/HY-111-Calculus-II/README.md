## 📖 Course Overview

HY-111 extends single-variable calculus into multivariable calculus and vector analysis in Euclidean spaces ($\mathbb{R}^n$), providing essential mathematical tools for computer graphics, machine learning, optimization, and scientific computing.

## 📋 Course Topics
* **Multivariable Functions & Geometry:** Euclidean metric spaces, topological concepts in $\mathbb{R}^n$, quadric surfaces, level curves and surfaces.
* **Partial Differentiation & Gradients:** Partial derivatives, directional derivatives, total differentials, gradient vector, tangent hyperplanes.
* **Optimization & Extrema:** Hessian matrix, local extrema classification, constrained optimization using Lagrange multipliers.
* **Multiple Integrals:** Double and triple integrals, Fubini's theorem, Jacobian determinants, transformation to polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates.
* **Vector Calculus:** Line integrals, surface integrals, Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem, and the Divergence (Gauss) theorem.
